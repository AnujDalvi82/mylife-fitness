> Always weigh dry grains and legumes raw whenever possible, or use standard measured cups once cooked. Free-pour cooking oil is the #1 reason cuts fail in Indian households!

### Oils, Fats & Condiments
- **Cooking Oil (Mustard, Olive, Groundnut, Ghee)**: 1 standard level teaspoon (5 ml) = **45 kcal, 5g fat**.

## 2. 7-Day Indian Cutting Sample Plan (~2,000 kcal / 165g Protein)

*Designed for ~79 kg individual in Phase 1 Cut. Adjust portions as needed.*

### Daily Meal Architecture (Macro Targets: ~2,000 kcal | 160-170g P | 50-55g F | 210-220g C)

#### 🌅 Breakfast (8:30 AM)
- **Option A (Pure Veg)**:
  - 40g Rolled Oats cooked in water + 1 scoop Whey Protein (stirred in after cooking) + 1 small banana (80g) or sliced apple (100g) + 5-6 crushed almonds (8g).
  - *Macros: ~360 kcal | 31g Protein | 46g Carbs | 6g Fat*
- **Option B (Egg-Friendly)**:
  - 2 Whole Eggs + 3 Egg Whites (bhurji or boiled) with 1 tsp oil + 2 slices 100% whole wheat bread or 1 medium phulka + tomato-onion salad.
  - *Macros: ~370 kcal | 30g Protein | 26g Carbs | 14g Fat*

#### 🍱 Lunch (1:00 PM)
- **Option A (High Protein Paneer & Dal Thali)**:
  - 100g Low-Fat Paneer or 100g Tofu (stir-fried with 1 tsp oil, capsicum, onion, jeera).
  - 1 medium katori Yellow Moong Dal or Toor Dal (150g cooked).
  - 2 Phulkas (no ghee) OR 150g cooked white/brown rice.
  - 1 large bowl Cucumber, Tomato & Carrot salad with lemon juice.
  - *Macros: ~520 kcal | 36g Protein | 62g Carbs | 14g Fat*
- **Option B (Soya Chunks Curry Bowl)**:
  - 45g Soya chunks cooked in onion-tomato-garlic gravy (using 1 tsp oil).
  - 150g Cooked Rice.
  - 150g Low-fat Curd (Dahi) with roasted jeera powder.
  - Large green salad.
  - *Macros: ~510 kcal | 38g Protein | 64g Carbs | 10g Fat*

#### ☕ Pre-Workout / Evening Snack (5:00 PM)
- 1 cup Black Coffee or Green Tea (no sugar).
- 1 medium Apple or 1 Banana + 1 scoop Whey Isolate/Concentrate in water OR 200g Low-fat Curd with roasted chana (25g).
- *Macros: ~220 kcal | 26g Protein | 25g Carbs | 2g Fat*

#### 🌙 Dinner (8:30 PM)
- **Option A (High Protein Soya / Paneer Sabzi)**:
  - 40g Soya chunks + 50g Low-fat Paneer prepared in light dry sabzi with mixed veggies (spinach/palak, beans, peas) with 1 tsp oil.
  - 2 Phulkas (no ghee).
  - 1 bowl clear vegetable soup or raw salad.
  - *Macros: ~490 kcal | 42g Protein | 50g Carbs | 13g Fat*
- **Option B (Chicken / Fish Alternative if non-veg)**:
  - 150g Grilled / pan-cooked Chicken breast in tandoori spices (1 tsp oil).
  - 1.5 Phulkas or 120g steamed rice.
  - 1 bowl steamed broccoli / green beans / mixed sabzi.
  - *Macros: ~460 kcal | 44g Protein | 36g Carbs | 10g Fat*

#### 🥛 Bedtime (Optional / Buffer - 10:30 PM)
- 150ml Warm Skimmed Milk or Turmeric Milk (Haldi doodh, no sugar) OR Chamomile tea + 4 almonds.
- *Macros: ~90 kcal | 5g Protein | 8g Carbs | 4g Fat*

**Daily Grand Total:** ~1,950 – 2,050 kcal | **162 – 172g Protein** | **48 – 54g Fat** | **205 – 220g Carbs**

## 3. Substitution & Micro-Adjustment Rules

### Exact Swap Equivalence
- **To replace 1 Scoop Whey Protein (25g P)**:
  - 45g Soya Chunks (Raw dry) OR
  - 120g Low-Fat Paneer OR
  - 170g Firm Tofu OR
  - 7 Egg Whites (if eating eggs) OR
  - 100g Raw Chicken Breast.
- **To replace 1 Medium Phulka (~100 kcal / 22g Carbs)**:
  - 75g Cooked Rice (~1/2 medium katori) OR
  - 25g Raw Rolled Oats OR
  - 1 Medium Boiled Potato (110g) OR
  - 30g Raw Poha.

### How to Cut or Add ~150 kcal
- **To reduce ~150 kcal (When hitting a 2-week weight plateau)**:
  - Cut 1 Phulka (-100 kcal) AND reduce cooking oil by 1 tsp (-45 kcal) = **-145 kcal**.
  - OR cut 110g cooked rice (-145 kcal).
- **To add ~150 kcal (When transitioning to Maintenance or during Lean Bulk)**:
  - Add 1 medium Phulka (+100 kcal) + 7 almonds (+45 kcal) = **+145 kcal**.
  - OR add 100g cooked rice (+130 kcal) + 1/2 tsp ghee (+22 kcal) = **+152 kcal**.

## 4. Indian Social & Dining Out Protocol
1. **Tandoori Over Curries**: Order Tandoori Paneer Tikka, Soya Chaap (ensure not deep-fried or mixed with maida), or Tandoori Chicken. Avoid butter-rich makhani gravies.
2. **Dal Tadka > Dal Makhani**: Dal tadka uses simple tempering with minimal butter/cream compared to cream-loaded Dal Makhani.
3. **Roti Buffer**: Request "Roti without butter / Bina butter ki Roti".
4. **Pre-load Protein**: Have 1 scoop of whey in water or 150g curd 30 mins before heading out to dinner to curb appetite.
