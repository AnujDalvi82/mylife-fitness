# Master Coaching & Periodization Guide

## Architecture Overview

This coaching program follows a 3-phase progression designed to strip body fat, protect lean muscle tissue, restore metabolic homeostasis, and then pack on lean mass sustainably:

```
[Phase 1: 8-Week Cut] ➔ [Phase 2: Maintenance Transition (2-3 Weeks)] ➔ [Phase 3: Lean Bulk (3-6+ Months)]
-400 to -600 kcal deficit     Step up +150-250 kcal/day to equilibrium    +200 to +300 kcal surplus
0.5 - 0.75 kg/week loss       Weight stable ±0.2-0.3 kg/week               0.25 - 0.5 kg/month gain
Protein: 2.0 - 2.2 g/kg       Protein: 2.0 g/kg                            Protein: 1.6 - 2.0 g/kg
```

---

## Phase 1: 8-Week Cutting Phase

### 1. Target Objectives
- **Target Loss Rate**: 0.50 – 0.75 kg per week (~4.0 – 6.0 kg over 8 weeks).
- **At 79.0 kg baseline**: 
  - Goal end weight: **73.0 – 75.0 kg**.
  - Calorie Deficit: **-400 to -600 kcal/day** below estimated Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).

### 2. Macro Framework (For 79 kg starting weight)
- **Protein**: **2.0 – 2.2 g/kg** ➔ **158g – 174g / day** (Crucial to spare lean muscle mass in a deficit).
- **Fats**: **0.6 – 0.8 g/kg** ➔ **48g – 63g / day** (Essential for testosterone, thyroid, and fat-soluble vitamin absorption).
- **Carbohydrates**: Remainder of calories ➔ Prioritize pre/post workout to preserve lifting intensity.

*Example baseline for ~2,000 kcal cut target:*
- **Protein**: 165g (~660 kcal / 33%)
- **Fats**: 52g (~468 kcal / 23%)
- **Carbs**: 218g (~872 kcal / 44%)
- *Total Calories*: ~2,000 kcal/day

### 3. Cutting Decision Rules (Data-Driven Adjustments)
Assess changes using **7-Day Rolling Morning Weight Averages**:
- **Scenario A (Plateau)**: If 7-day average weight is flat (< 0.2 kg change) for **2 consecutive weeks**:
  - *Action*: Reduce intake by **100–150 kcal/day** (e.g., reduce 1 roti or 1.5 tsp cooking oil) OR add **20–30 mins low-intensity steady-state cardio (LISS) 2–3x/week** / add +1,500 daily steps.
- **Scenario B (Too Rapid Loss)**: If losing **> 1.0 kg/week for 2 consecutive weeks**:
  - *Risk*: Muscle loss, metabolic slowdown, lethargy, binge triggers.
  - *Action*: Add **100–200 kcal/day** from complex carbs (e.g., +1 medium roti or +30g raw oats).
- **Scenario C (On Track)**: Losing **0.4 – 0.8 kg/week**:
  - *Action*: Maintain current intake and output. Do not tamper with success.

## Phase 2: Transition to Maintenance (Reverse/Stabilization)

### 1. Purpose
Prevent immediate rebound fat gain, normalize ghrelin/leptin (hunger hormones), restore gym performance, and pinpoint true maintenance calories before bulking.

### 2. Protocol
- Immediately post-cut, add **+150 to +250 kcal/day** (primarily from carbohydrates).
- Hold for 7–10 days and observe average morning weight.
- If weight remains flat or increases by less than 0.3 kg (water retention glycogen refill is normal up to ~0.5-1 kg in week 1):
  - Add another **+100 to +150 kcal/day**.
- **Indicators of True Maintenance**:
  - Scale weight stable within ±0.2–0.3 kg over 2 weeks.
  - Recovery between sets improves, daytime energy rebounds, and constant food cravings vanish.

---

## Phase 3: Lean Bulk (Hypertrophy Focus)

### 1. Target Objectives
- **Target Rate of Gain**: **0.25 – 0.50 kg per month** (controlled to minimize fat accumulation).
- **Calorie Surplus**: **+200 to +300 kcal/day** above verified maintenance.
- **Duration**: 3 to 6+ months.

### 2. Macro Framework
- **Protein**: **1.6 – 2.0 g/kg/day** (Sufficient for muscle protein synthesis in a surplus).
- **Fats**: **0.7 – 1.0 g/kg/day** (Hormone health and joint lubrication).
- **Carbs**: Remainder of calories (Fueling heavy progressive overload in the gym).

### 3. Bulking Decision Rules
- **Scenario A (Under-eating)**: Average weight does not increase over **3 consecutive weeks**:
  - *Action*: Increase intake by **+100 to +150 kcal/day** (e.g., 20g almonds/walnuts + 1 extra cup cooked rice).
- **Scenario B (Spillover / Fast Fat Gain)**: Average weight increases **> 0.75 kg in a single month**:
  - *Action*: Reduce surplus by **100–150 kcal/day** to prevent unnecessary fat gain.
- **Scenario C (Training Stall)**: If a compound lift weight is stuck for 3 consecutive weeks:
  - *Action*: Implement back-off sets (10% drop in weight for +2 reps) or cycle into a 6–8 rep microcycle before testing loads again.

## Golden Rules for Indian Diets & Lifestyle
1. **Never guess cooking oil**: In Indian cooking, free-pouring oil easily adds 300–500 hidden kcal. Always measure with a 5ml teaspoon (1 tsp = ~45 kcal).
2. **Prioritize complete protein distribution**: Space protein into 3–4 meals of 35–45g each to maximize muscle protein synthesis (MPS).
3. **Fiber and Vegetable Minimums**: Include at least 2 distinct vegetable servings (raw cucumbers, tomatoes, bhindi, gobhi, palak) and 1–2 whole fruits daily (apples, papaya, guava).
4. **Hydration**: Minimum 3.0 to 3.5 liters of water daily.
