# Phase 1: Cutting Workout Program & Cardio Protocol

## Training Philosophy During a Cut
> [!IMPORTANT]
> The primary purpose of lifting weights during a cutting phase is to **signal the body to preserve muscle tissue**. You cannot lift with "light weights and 30 reps to burn fat" — you must train with high intensity (within 1–3 Reps In Reserve) so the body realizes muscle is essential.

## Comprehensive Warm-Up & Mobility Protocol (8–10 Mins)
1. **General Warm-up (3 mins)**: Incline treadmill walk or light rowing machine to raise core body temperature.
2. **Dynamic Shoulder & Thoracic Mobility (3 mins)**:
   - Band pull-aparts / Face pulls: 2 sets × 15 reps
   - Arm circles & Shoulder dislocates (using light band or PVC pipe): 10 reps each direction
   - Cat-Cow & World's Greatest Stretch: 5 reps per side
3. **Lower Body & Hip Mobility (3 mins)**:
   - Deep bodyweight squat hold (Goblet squat position with elbows pushing knees out): 45 seconds
   - Glute bridges & 90/90 hip switches: 10 reps per side
4. **Exercise-Specific Warm-up Sets**:
   - Set 1: 50% working weight × 8 reps (fast and controlled)
   - Set 2: 75% working weight × 4 reps (prep nervous system)
   - *Then begin working sets.*

## Progression Rules (Double Progression Model)
1. **The System**: Each exercise has a designated rep range (e.g., 8–10 reps).
2. **When to Add Weight**: When you can perform all sets at the **top end of the rep range** with crisp form and 1–2 RIR in hand, increase the load for the next session:
   - Upper body: +1.0 to 2.5 kg total.
   - Lower body: +2.5 to 5.0 kg total.
3. **If You Miss the Bottom of the Range**: Keep the weight the same; fight for +1 rep on your next session.

## Cardio Protocol (Fat Loss Accelerator & Recovery Preserver)
- **Primary Goal**: Increase daily energy expenditure without impairing leg recovery or strength progression.
- **Protocol 1 (Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis - NEAT)**:
  - **Baseline Daily Steps**: **8,000 – 10,000 steps/day**. This burns ~300-400 kcal without spiking cortisol or hunger.
- **Protocol 2 (LISS Conditioning)**:
  - **Type**: Incline Treadmill Walking (Speed: 4.5–5.0 km/h, Incline: 8–12%) or stationary cycle.
  - **Frequency & Duration**: 2–3 sessions per week, **20–25 minutes** post-workout or on rest days.
  - **Avoid**: Excessive HIIT sprints or long grueling runs during a caloric deficit, as they induce high central nervous system (CNS) fatigue and increase injury risk.

## Fatigue Management, Joint Pain & Deloads
- **Signs of Excessive Accumulated Fatigue**:
  - Main lift strength drops by >10% over 2 consecutive sessions.
  - Persistent joint aches (tendonitis in elbows, knees, or shoulders).
  - Resting heart rate elevated, sleep fragmentation.
- **The Deload Protocol (Every 5th–7th Week)**:
  - Cut total sets by 50% (e.g., do 2 sets instead of 3–4).
  - Reduce weight to 70–80% of normal working weights.
  - Keep diet at maintenance or mild deficit to accelerate recovery.
- **Joint Pain Substitutions**:
  - Shoulder pain on Barbell Bench ➔ Switch to Neutral-Grip Dumbbell Press or Incline DB Press.
  - Lower back fatigue on Squats ➔ Switch to Hack Squats or Belt Squats.
  - Knee pain on Leg Extensions ➔ Switch to Step-Ups or Spanish Squats.
